YAPPIE

Web chat rules

Web chat is for adults 18 and older. Harassment, threats, hate, unwanted sexual content, spam, and scams are prohibited. Keep contact details and other personal information out of stranger chats.

Leave at any time. Use Block to prevent another match with that guest session, or Report to send recent messages to moderators and leave the chat.

Guest sessions and messages

Yappie uses a temporary session cookie and a protected network identifier to run matching and limit abuse. Recent text is available to moderators through a report during the chat and for 15 minutes afterward. Reports may retain recent text and moderation identifiers for up to 30 days. Voice calls use a relay and Yappie does not record them.
