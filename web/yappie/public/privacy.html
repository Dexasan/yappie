Beta privacy notice

Account and profile

We process your email, authentication records, and age eligibility to operate your account. Your email and birth date are not shown publicly. Your profile, posts, replies, follows, and Club activity can be visible to other signed-in users.

Conversations and safety

Messages and room participation are processed to deliver conversations and investigate reports. Live audio is transmitted through the realtime provider; the app is not designed to record calls. Blocks, reports, and limited technical records support moderation and reliability.

Discovery and alerts

Interests, languages, follows, Club topics, and likes help order Discover posts. If you enable notifications, we store a device token and alert preferences. Unsent drafts stay on your device. Limited usage events exclude message bodies and typed content.

Stranger chats

Website and Android users share Cloudflare-hosted stranger matching, text delivery, and voice signaling. Temporary session cookies, protected network identifiers, and app language and block preferences support matching and safety. Recent text is retained for the chat and up to 15 minutes afterward. Reports may retain conversation excerpts for up to 30 days. Blocking a stranger prevents another match with that session for 24 hours. Existing account blocks are also used for signed-in app matching. Voice calls are relayed and are not recorded by Yappie. See the chat rules for details.

Website visitor estimate

The public visitor counter estimates unique active browsers from the time counting is enabled; it is not an exact count of people or a count of people currently online. A browser qualifies after a real interaction and eight visible seconds on a public page. Obvious automation is filtered, but sophisticated bots can still qualify. Quick visits, disabled cookies or JavaScript, shared networks, and privacy settings may be missed. Different devices or cleared cookies can produce another count.

After an interaction, a first-party, secure HttpOnly cookie stores a random, signed visitor identifier for up to one year to avoid counting return visits again. The counter stores this identifier until expiry, an aggregate total, and its start date. A daily protected network identifier is used for abuse limits and removed after expiry, normally within three days. The counter does not store raw IP addresses, user agents, page histories, referring websites, chat messages, typed content, interests, account IDs, or emails. It does not count the embedded Android chat or authentication pages. Browsers sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not registered. Clearing this site's cookies removes the visitor identifier from your browser, and may make a later visit count again. Anonymous aggregate totals are retained.

Services

Supabase handles accounts and app data. Resend delivers account emails. Cloudflare hosts this site, shared stranger chats, and live audio infrastructure. Expo distributes the beta build.

Your choices

In Yappie, you can edit your profile, manage message and notification preferences, block people, or delete your account. See beta support for help.
