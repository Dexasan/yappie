Beta privacy notice

Account and profile

We process your email, authentication records, and age eligibility to operate your account. Your email and birth date are not shown publicly. Your profile, posts, replies, follows, and Club activity can be visible to other signed-in users.

Conversations and safety

Messages and room participation are processed to deliver conversations and investigate reports. Live audio is transmitted through the realtime provider; the app is not designed to record calls. Blocks, reports, and limited technical records support moderation and reliability.

Discovery and alerts

Interests, languages, follows, Club topics, and likes help order Discover posts. If you enable notifications, we store a device token and alert preferences. Unsent drafts stay on your device. Limited usage events exclude message bodies and typed content.

Services

Supabase handles accounts and app data. Resend delivers account emails. Cloudflare hosts this site and provides live audio infrastructure. Expo distributes the beta build.

Your choices

In Yappie, you can edit your profile, manage message and notification preferences, block people, or delete your account. See beta support for help.
