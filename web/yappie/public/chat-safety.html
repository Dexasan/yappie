Chatting with strangers: privacy and safety

You decide what to share and when to leave.

Yappie is a one-to-one text chat service for adults 18 and older. It has rules and reporting tools, but cannot guarantee how another person will behave. Treat information from a stranger as unverified, including claims about their age or identity.

Keep identifying details out of the conversation

You do not need to give your real name, email address, or phone number to use browser chat. Avoid sharing your address, school, workplace, live location, account passwords, verification codes, or financial details. A social media handle can reveal more about you than you expect.

Small details can add up. A neighbourhood, a shift time, and a photo description may identify you even when you never state your name. You can talk about a hobby or a favourite film without giving an exact location or routine.

Know what anonymous guest chat means

Browser participants appear as Stranger. Anonymity in the conversation does not mean complete anonymity from the service. Yappie uses a temporary session cookie and protected network identifiers to make matching work and limit abuse. It does not promise that the other person cannot copy, screenshot, or share a message.

Recent text is retained during the chat and for up to 15 minutes afterward. A report may retain recent messages and moderation identifiers for up to 30 days. See the chat rules and privacy notice for the service's data practices.

Recognise pressure and scams

  • Do not send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, account credentials, or verification codes to someone you met in a random chat.
  • Be cautious about urgent stories, investment offers, prizes, and requests to install software.
  • Avoid unexpected links and downloads. A familiar-looking name does not prove a link is genuine.
  • If someone pressures you to move immediately to another service, share intimate material, or keep a conversation secret, you can leave.

Leave, block, or report

Leave: Use Leave chat at the top of the conversation. You do not need the other person's permission. On a keyboard, Esc opens the leave confirmation.

Block: Open Conversation options during a connected chat, then choose Block. This prevents another match with that session for 24 hours. It is a session block, not a guarantee against every future account or session that person might use.

Report: Open Conversation options and choose Report. Select the reason and choose Report and leave. Your reason and recent messages are sent for moderator review. Yappie does not promise an immediate response or continuous monitoring of every chat.

Respect the person on the other side

Harassment, threats, hate, unwanted sexual content, spam, and scams are prohibited. If the other person does not want to answer a question or continue talking, respect that choice. Do not use stranger chat if you are under 18.

For a technical problem, use Yappie support. Do not put private messages, personal details, or account links into a public bug report.
