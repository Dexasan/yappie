About Yappie

Free text conversations with someone you have not met.

Yappie connects adults for one-to-one text chat. Open the website, choose a language and an optional interest, confirm you are 18 or older, and join the matching queue. Browser guests do not need to create an account or download an app.

How matching works

Website guests and eligible Android beta users share the stranger-chat pool. Matching prefers a shared interest when an eligible partner is waiting. The website offers a choice of chat language. A match depends on another person being online and available; an interest selection does not guarantee a partner.

What is available

The public website offers free text chat, optional interests, a language selector, and leave, block, and report controls. Browser participants appear as Stranger. Public stranger voice and video calls are not currently available.

Beta status

Yappie is in beta. At quiet times you may need to wait or try again later. The Android app is also in beta; there is no public store download linked here yet. The website works independently of the app.

Safety and privacy

Stranger chat is for adults 18 and older. Read the chat rules, safety guide, and privacy notice before joining. Guest chat is not a promise of complete anonymity, and no platform can guarantee a stranger's behaviour.

Help and feedback

Use Yappie support for technical problems and beta account help. Report an abusive conversation using the report control inside the chat so moderators receive the relevant context.
