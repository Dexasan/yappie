Beta support

Email links

Open the latest verification or reset email on the phone where you started. If the link expired, request a new one in Yappie.

Android beta installation

If you’re part of the Android beta, ask the person who invited you for the latest build.

Safety and your account

Use the in-app report and block controls for safety issues. You can delete your account in Yappie’s Account settings.

Report a technical problem

For a general bug, use the public issue tracker. Do not post email addresses, verification links, screenshots of private messages, or other personal data. For private help during the beta, contact the person who invited you.
